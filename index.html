I met my brother, Ian Nyakundi, back in a classroom in 2018. Neither of us knew it then, but from that day forward, life was purely motion. We've watched seasons shift overnight—feeling the weight of the pressure, taking losses in silence, navigating the confusion of growing up, and eventually securing those few, hard-earned wins that reminded us exactly why we never quit.

We didn't just build a business this past year; we recreated our entire pack from the ground up. Taking on the mantle of CEO, with Ian standing right beside me as COO, wasn't just a title—it was a promise. We learned what true loyalty means when your back is against the wall. We've shared the kind of trauma, the late-night laughs, the crushing setbacks, and the relentless ambition that permanently rewires who you are.

Some of those years altered us completely. Some of those moments came close to breaking us. But instead of folding under the pressure, we just kept evolving.

That's why turning 20 doesn't just feel like a birthday. It feels like a transition. Moving out of the noise and into pure purpose. Less time dreaming. More time building.
